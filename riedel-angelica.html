About the Author

Reidel Angelica Lerado is a 23-year-old writer and author on Wattpad under the pen name riedel_angelica. She graduated from Mindanao State University - Gensan and started writing back in high school, eventually carrying the hobby with her even after college.

She writes stories across different genres, including Taguan ng Anak, mafia stories, and school-themed romance. As a writer, she enjoys creating stories that keep readers guessing. She is the kind of author who will confuse you first before slowly revealing why everything happened. For her, the journey toward the truth is just as important as the truth itself.

For Riedel, libangan lamang ang pagsusulat. She never started writing for fame, nor does she write simply to please what readers expect. She writes the stories she wants to tell, in the way she wants to tell them.

Her philosophy is simple: If you enjoy the story, stay. If you don't, you're free to leave.

After all, she believes that stories aren't meant to please everyone. Sometimes, they're meant to challenge you, confuse you, frustrate you, and make you question everything until the pieces finally fall into place.
