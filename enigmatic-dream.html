Enigmatic Dream

About the Book

Calliope “Calli” Agner joins a mysterious survival game to save her family. Winning could solve everything, but losing could cost her life.

In a strange realm filled with danger and magic, she's paired with Brennon Cardinal, a man she's only met in her dreams. As they fight to survive deadly trials, Calli learns their connection is more than coincidence.

But in a game where only one can win, trust is a risk... and love might be the most dangerous move of all.
