My Sweet Boy

by QuietSpectra

About the Book

Meet Archimedes Christian "Archie" Royales—charming, admirable, a green flag, adorable, and malambing—that's what he is. Living with Asperger's Syndrome, a high-functioning form of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), he is struggling with the world where he was born while enjoying the things he loves doing. As a person on the spectrum, lagi siyang minamahal ng mga taong nakapaligid sa kaniya, lalo na because of his personality.

Aside from being a Grade 12 ICT student, he is also working part-time as a cashier at his older sister's café—but his disability is not a barrier for him to survive, ika nga nila. This is how he balances his work and studies, and it is enough for him to thrive. Anak rin siya ng isang nurse at isang SNED teacher, na isa ring Autism Advocate na nagbabahagi ng awareness sa kanilang community.

At dahil marami na rin kasing misconceptions about his condition, he still always stands his ground, at handa na rin siyang tanggapin ang kanyang sarili with the best of his effort. Inside the home, school, community, church, among friends, and in other places, his future is always waiting for him.

Bilang isang autistic, nakikita niya ang kaniyang sarili na "Different, Not Less," dahil kaya na niyang mamuhay on his own when the time comes that his future welcomes him with open arms.

Sa mundong ginagalawan niya, haharapin niya ang mga challenges na kinakailangan niyang harapin—judgment from other people, bullying, meltdowns, sensory issues, mga myths about his condition, and other challenges that affect him as he explores the world where he was born. Pero sa tulong ng mga kapamilya at kaibigan, he did everything he could—nalampasan niya ang mga pagsubok na kinakaharap niya lately.

How will he take full responsibility as he is now enjoying his life to the fullest, now that he is a grown-up adult? Paano maibibigay ni Archie ang kaniyang best na mapagtagumpayan niya ang mga pagsubok na pinagdadaanan niya?

Will he get the chance to be himself?
