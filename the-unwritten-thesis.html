The Unwritten Thesis

About the Book

Luella Rose Jacinta has always lived in the shadow of her twin sister, Luenna Ambrose Jacinta. While Luenna is loved and recognized as the Jacintas' daughter, Luella has been kept hidden inside their mansion. Almost no one knows she exists.

When Luenna runs away to escape her arranged marriage to Davian Terron Salvatore, Luella is forced to take her place to protect their family's business. She becomes Davian's bride, pretending to be the sister everyone knows.

But what happens when Luenna comes back and takes her place again?

Will Luella be able to let go of the life she was never meant to have? And can she walk away from a love that was never meant for her?
