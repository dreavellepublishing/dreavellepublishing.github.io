About the Author

Jelo Dayday, also known as QuietSpectra, is a rising Wattpad author and an aspiring published author. He was born in Pasig City on July 17, 2004.

He began writing on March 16, 2024, but lost his spark on November 10, 2024. He returned to writing on January 1, 2025, and continued working on his first novel, My Sweet Boy: An Archie Royales Fanfiction.

He completed his secondary studies at the ICCT Colleges Taytay Satellite Campus. On Wattpad, he is known as QuietSpectra. He was previously known as JeloStories, his former Wattpad username, which he also uses on GoodNovel.
